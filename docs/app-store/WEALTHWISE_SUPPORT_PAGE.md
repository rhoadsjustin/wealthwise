## Troubleshooting

### Insights are still setting up

The first time you use the Insights feature, WealthWise may download on-device models. Keep the app open until setup finishes, then try again.

### Face ID or Touch ID is unavailable

Make sure biometrics are enabled on your iPhone and that you turned on App Lock inside WealthWise.

### My data looks missing

WealthWise stores its core budgeting data locally on your device. If you removed the app or reset local storage, that data may no longer be available.

### I found a bug

Email `hello@superdope.company` with:

- your iPhone model
- your iOS version
- what you were doing when the issue happened
- screenshots if available
