# WealthWise Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 16, 2026

## Overview

WealthWise is a local-first budgeting app. Its core budgeting data is stored on your device so you can track spending, savings, bills, and debts without creating an online account.

## Information We Store

- Budgeting data you enter, including transactions, categories, budgets, savings goals, bills, debt records, and profile preferences
- App settings stored locally on your device, such as onboarding completion, username, monthly income, app lock preference, and optional passcode
- A preference flag for biometric unlock stored in secure device storage

## Biometrics

WealthWise can use Face ID or Touch ID to unlock the app when you enable App Lock. WealthWise does not receive or store your biometric data. Biometric matching is handled by Apple on your device.

## On-Device AI Features

The Insights feature and category suggestions use on-device models. The first time you use certain insight features, WealthWise may download model files to your device. Those model files are stored locally to support private, on-device processing.

## Analytics

WealthWise may send limited product analytics and app performance events to our analytics provider to help us understand feature usage and app stability. We do not use this analytics setup for cross-app or cross-website tracking.

## What We Do Not Do

- We do not require an online account to use core budgeting features
- We do not sell your personal information
- We do not upload biometric data
- We do not ship bank account linking or automatic bank import in this MVP release

## Data Retention And Control

Because WealthWise stores its core budgeting data locally, you can remove most stored data by deleting entries in the app or removing the app from your device. Some preferences may also be cleared from within the app.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact `privacy@superdope.company`.
